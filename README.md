01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

MKR-SPARK 26 is an independent AI research and development project focused on exploring the foundations of a lightweight artificial intelligence system.

The project aims to investigate how AI models can become more accessible, efficient, and independent without requiring expensive hardware or powerful GPUs.

Rather than depending entirely on existing AI services, MKR-SPARK explores an independent development direction built around efficient computation, intelligent memory, and external knowledge retrieval.

02 / THE VISION

<div align="center">INTELLIGENCE WITHOUT HARDWARE LIMITS

</div>We believe artificial intelligence should be accessible to everyone.

Our long-term vision is to develop an AI system capable of operating on ordinary computers, with a strong focus on:

- Efficient CPU-based execution.
- Independent model development.
- Persistent memory and knowledge retrieval.
- Arabic and English language capabilities.
- Reduced hardware requirements.
- Expandable and modular architecture.
